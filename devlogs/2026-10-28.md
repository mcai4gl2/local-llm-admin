## Splash Phase 2 owning-wrapper implementation in progress

### Problem/Goal
Continue the approved Phase 2 implementation after the validated profile/ownership
foundation. Own the controller/native bundle and enforce reciprocal host CLI exclusions.

### Solution/Implementation
Added the standalone start/stop/status helper and real-zsh wrapper, explicit runtime audit
receipt, clean private launch environment and pinned 32K/reasoning-on/cache/idle settings.
Ownership checks verify executable/root, launch identity, PID and creation time; recorded
children remain tracked after parent death. Signals revalidate generations, unrecorded
orphans block activation, and status reads do not rewrite the ownership record.

maintenance.lock acquisition, and reciprocal Splash guards in existing owning wrappers.
MLX keeps its original reservation. Initial subshell reservations made zsh wait for
background engines; moving to the wrapper process and global cleanup traps fixes this.
Untruncated ps inspection avoids losing ownership markers. Process-table failure is unknown,
and private credentials/profile failures leave readiness unavailable rather than crashing.

## Lessons Learned
Zsh function exits and background jobs require cleanup traps at the wrapper level; a
subshell changes lifetime behavior. Process-command truncation can invalidate otherwise
correct ownership checks. A missing process table must never be treated as no running owner.
