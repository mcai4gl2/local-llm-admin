## Plan: Strata engine with Qwen3.8-Flash-Next

### Problem/Goal
Check whether Qwen3.8-Flash-Next IQ3 can run on the Mac mini through the Strata engine, or
whether Q2 is needed, and plan adding Strata to the admin.

### Solution/Implementation
Researched Strata's Mac docs and Unsloth's quant table. On a 64 GB Mac, Strata recommends
Q2_0 (37.6 GB pack, resident); IQ3_XXS (47.0 GB) exceeds the 70% resident cap and would be
streamed, a mode measured only at 32 GB and requiring the internal SSD. Wrote
`plans/20261015_strata-qwen38-flash-next.md`: Q2_0 first, IQ3_XXS behind a measured gate,
Strata as a third exclusive engine driven by a host wrapper.

Plan only; no code changed.
